Automated Scenario Generation Using Halton Sequences for the Verification of Autonomous Vehicle Behavior in Simulation Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering Manual scenario creation is time consuming and does not guarantee scenario coverage. Pseudo-random scenario generation is a faster option, but still does not ensure coverage of the state space. Therefore, this study proposes the use of Halton sequences to automatically generate scenarios for autonomous vehicle testing in simulation. These tests are carried out in the CARLA simulation environment on an open source, published driving model called “Learning by Cheating” which takes place as the system-under-test.
