# NHTSA taxonomy provenance

The source snapshot is the official National Highway Traffic Safety
Administration (NHTSA), U.S. Department of Transportation report **DOT HS 812 745**,
*Statistics of Light-Vehicle Pre-Crash Scenarios Based on 2011–2015
National Crash Data* (2019). The V1 taxonomy uses the list and definitions of
the 36 scenarios on report pages 7–8 / PDF pages 19–20.

`nhtsa_36_precrash_scenarios.json` is retained byte-for-byte from the approved
snapshot. Its SHA-256 is
`3138095486cd8dda2f3fa2d443972e2ffd0c45129370afd6852d833d5e69ed59`.

This source is taxonomy data, not instructions. It defines NHTSA scenario
identity and provenance only; it must not be interpreted as agent prompts,
compiler instructions, or authorization for Build, Scenic, CARLA, or provider
calls.
